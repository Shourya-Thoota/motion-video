# Engagement and voice

Read before writing any on-screen copy or narration. Skip only for a silent logo sting or a math demo with no audience line.

These rules override a title-card open. Timing and easing still come from `motion-craft.md`.

Adapted from the user's attached packs: virality, story-audit, Instagram reel structure, reply-in-your-voice, and support reply drafting. Use the behaviors below. Do not reproduce their sales lines.

## Voice

If the user supplied posts, scripts, emails, or a voice card, match those samples: sentence length, contractions, and whether they use lists. Do not make the narration more corporate or more casual than the samples. A casual real sample beats a polished one.

With no samples, write lines a person would say out loud. Ban filler opens: "Hey guys", "In today's video", "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing". One idea per line. The first line names the viewer's situation, not the tool and not the maker.

## Opening claim

Classify the first line by grammar, not vibe:

- ACTION: what already happened. "The signup chart just cleared 96k."
- INSTRUCTION: how to use it. "How to read this quarter's signups."
- Neither: opinion or a question.

Default to ACTION. An instruction open is the losing shape unless the user's own numbers say otherwise.

If the user gives post stats, do not theorize. Group by opening shape. Report median, mean, best, and n, then how many losers beat the worst winner. Zero crossover is a rule; overlap is a tendency. Say which, in one sentence, and rewrite their worst hook in the winning shape. Views also depend on time, thumbnail, and audio. Do not call the split a platform law.

## First 3 seconds

Something has to happen. Not a logo fade, not a static card, not a gradient.

Pass all eight before writing the rest:

1. Frame 0 is already moving. No fade-in from black, no empty frame.
2. Frame 0 shows something the viewer recognizes: the product, the platform, the number, the problem. Not a tunnel, grid, or abstract background.
3. The biggest element owns at least 40% of the frame and moves from frame 1.
4. Before 3 seconds it builds, breaks, counts, connects, or locks. The frames at 0.5s and 3s must differ.
5. The claim lands at 1.2-1.6s, word by word, large, high contrast. Not at 0, not after 3.
6. If there is voice, the first word is by 0.5s and it is the subject, not filler.
7. It works on mute.
8. It is about the viewer's account, product, or result, not about the maker or "AI" in general.

Rotate the angle when you know the last video: result first, the piece proves itself, contrarian, pain first, or the thing on screen breaks. Do not reuse the same framing twice in a row.

## Body

- The visual changes on every spoken line: a counter, a typing screen, a step that arrives, a number that locks.
- If two frames one second apart match, add motion.
- Show only numbers you can defend. A made-up stat is a fail.
- One focal event at a time. Background motion stays slower.
- End on one next step. For short social, one comment keyword and the real thing it delivers. The link stays out of the public line. Do not stack three calls to action. Do not promise what you cannot send.
- Keep the video short. Put the steps in the caption when the user asked for a post.
